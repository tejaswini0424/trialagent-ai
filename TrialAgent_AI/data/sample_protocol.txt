SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DATA — NOT A REAL CLINICAL TRIAL
Study title: TEAL-01 Blood Pressure Feasibility Study
Sponsor: Synthetic Research University
Phase II
Objective: Assess reduction in blood pressure at week 12.
Study design: Randomized double-blind placebo-controlled parallel study.
Population: Adults with hypertension.
Sample size: 120 participants.
Duration: 12 weeks treatment and 4 weeks follow-up.
Start date: 2027-04-01
End date: 2027-03-01
Inclusion: Age 18 to 65 years.
Inclusion: Adults with hypertension and adequate organ function.
Inclusion: Age 18 to 65 years.
Exclusion: Hypertension.
Exclusion: Pregnant participants.
Treatment arm A: Synthetic compound 5 mg once daily for 12 weeks.
Treatment arm B: Matching placebo once daily for 12 weeks.
Primary endpoint: Change in systolic blood pressure at week 12.
Secondary endpoint: Change in quality of life at week 12.
Safety monitoring: Weekly symptom review and laboratory monitoring.
Adverse event reporting: Investigators report adverse events in case report forms.
Statistical analysis: Intention-to-treat comparison of mean change.
Statistical appendix: Enroll 150 participants.
Primary endpoint: Change in diastolic blood pressure at week 8.
Informed consent: Signed consent obtained before study procedures.
See section 14 for stopping criteria.
Deliberate gaps: committee membership, serious event timelines, ethics approval, data management and amendment procedures are not supplied.
